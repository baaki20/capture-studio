Source code offer for FFmpeg
============================

Capture Studio distributes FFmpeg executables under the GNU General Public License,
version 3 or later. In accordance with section 6 of that licence, the Corresponding Source
for the exact build we ship is available as follows.

1. The FFmpeg source. The bundled build identifies itself as the revision printed by
   `ffmpeg -version` (for example "n9.0.2-35-g9a9b70bb8c": 35 commits after tag n9.0.2 on
   the release/9.0 branch, commit 9a9b70bb8c). The source for that revision is available at

       https://git.ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.git
       https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/commit/<revision>

   The revision, branch and release tag of every bundled platform are also recorded in
   `bin/<platform>-<arch>/BUILD-INFO.json` and in `scripts/ffmpeg-manifest.json` of the
   Capture Studio source tree.

2. The build scripts and the sources of every third-party library compiled into the
   executables, with the exact versions used, are defined by the BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds
   repository at the release tag recorded in BUILD-INFO.json:

       https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds/tree/<release tag>

3. Written offer. For at least three years after we last distribute a given version of
   Capture Studio, and for as long as we offer spare parts or support for it, we will give
   anyone who asks a complete machine-readable copy of the Corresponding Source of the
   FFmpeg executables included in that version, on a medium customarily used for software
   interchange, for a charge no more than our cost of physically performing the
   distribution. Requests: open an issue in the Capture Studio project's issue tracker or
   use the contact address published with the Capture Studio release you received.

Capture Studio's own source code is available under GPL-3.0-or-later from the same place
you obtained Capture Studio.
